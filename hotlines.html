    Las Piñas City Emergency Protocols

    Disaster preparedness tips provided by Las Piñas DRRMO

    Severe Flooding / Typhoon Protocol

    Turn off the main electrical breaker if water reaches ground sockets. Evacuate early if residing near Zapote River or coastal areas.

    Earthquake Action ("Drop, Cover & Hold")

    Take shelter beneath a sturdy table. Keep clear of heavy glass windows and brick walls. Evacuate to open barangay fields only after shaking stops.

    Fire Escape Protocol

    Crawl low under smoke where air is cleaner. Never use elevators. Test door handles for heat before opening.
